Who we are

Still the same van. Slightly better ladders.

Hatherley Roofing Ltd has been on the same industrial estate off the A40 since 1987. Dave answers the phone. Josh does the measuring. The Transit has had three engines and one working indicator.

  • CSCS-carded team — no cash-in-hand cousins on the roof
  • We scaffold properly. We do not “make do with a ladder and hope”
  • Waste taken to the tip, not left in a heap for the bin men
  • Quotes in writing. The number on the quote is the number you pay

What we do

Roofs, and the bits that pretend to be roofs

From a slipped tile after a Sunday storm to a full tear-off. If it sits on top of a house in Bucks, we have probably sworn at it.

New roofs

Slate, clay and concrete tile. Full tear-off and re-batten.

Repairs

Leaks, slipped tiles, flashing, storm damage — sorted fast.

Flat roofs

EPDM rubber and GRP. The 1970s felt is retired, with honours.

Chimneys

Repointing, pots, lead soakers. Birds evicted, diplomatically.

Fascias & gutters

uPVC fascias, soffits, and gutters that actually fall the right way.

Emergency

Tarps, temporary repairs, and a kettle if it’s really coming down.
