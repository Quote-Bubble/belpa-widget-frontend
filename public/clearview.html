The important bit

Why we will not jet wash your roof

Plenty of people will. It looks dramatic on a video and it is quicker for them. It is also how roofs get damaged.

What a pressure washer actually does up there

Concrete tiles are made with a coloured surface layer. Blast it off and the tile goes patchy and porous, holds more water, and grows moss back faster than before it was cleaned. On older clay and slate the risk is the water, not the colour — driven uphill under the laps it soaks the felt and the batten, and you find out in the loft a month later. It also fires the moss into the gutters, which then need clearing anyway.

Soft washing takes longer and does not look as good on video. It is the only way we will do it.
